- **0:00–0:30 (Intro & Owner Login):** Role-based access, dashboard overview.

- **1:00–1:30 (Care & Commerce):** Vet appointment booking, AI Chatbot interaction, mock e-commerce checkout.
- **1:30–2:15 (Vet Login):** Dashboard queue, patient history lookup, structured treatment logging.
- **2:15–3:00 (Shelter Login):** Adoption listings view, application coordination drawer, care logs.
- **3:00–3:30 (Innovation Showcase):** Trigger Emergency SOS map, click PDF Medical Passport generation, close video.
